About us

Benvenuti!

Welcome to 21 Avenue de la Liberté, the first Maestri della Pasta Chic address.

Maestri della Pasta began with an idea from Loris Amistà: bringing real Italian pasta to the world without losing its soul. After more than ten years of work, we have served over 790,000 guests in three countries since 2017. Today this story finds its home here and takes a seat at the table, with table service, carefully plated dishes and time to enjoy them.

It all starts with the ingredients. They come 100% from Italy, chosen from small family producers, and become dishes that follow time-honoured recipes. At the heart of our menu are fresh pasta and our pizzas. Around them, antipasti to share and artisanal desserts. To go with them, a selection of Italian wines for lunch and dinner.

The spirit of the 21 stays the same, with a new signature.

Buon appetito!

The restaurant

Lunch and dinner

Al 21 Italian Restaurant, Lounge & Wine Bar is a welcoming place to enjoy quality Italian food, and a meeting point for relaxed, easy-going and fun moments.

The stylish, tastefully furnished space can be enjoyed indoors or out on the terrace. And alongside our fresh pasta there is pizza, made with a dough created by Gianni Calaon, world champion of pizza in pala: light, crisp and full of flavour.
